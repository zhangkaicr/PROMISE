# Project Description（项目说明）​
- This project focuses on the construction and validation of a dynamic prediction model based on Bayesian joint models for patients undergoing radical resection of hepatocellular carcinoma. The key details of the project are as follows:（本项目聚焦于基于贝叶斯联合模型的肝细胞肝癌根治性切除术后动态预测模型的构建与验证，项目核心详情如下：）
​
1. Estimated Sample Size: 3,000–5,000 cases. The sample size from a single center is required to be more than 200 patients.（1. 预计样本量：3000-5000例。要求单中心的样本量需大于200例次患者。）​
2. Required Data Modalities: Structured data and raw imaging data (imaging data will be reserved as an optional modality for subsequent analyses). Longitudinal data with multiple follow-ups is required.（2. 所需数据模态：结构化数据、影像学原始数据（影像学数据作为后续分析备选项）。需要多次随访的纵向数据。）
​
# Cooperation and Win-Win Mechanism（合作共赢机制）​
1. Both parties shall give full play to their respective resource advantages (including but not limited to data, technology, and human resources) to jointly promote the smooth progress of the project and ensure the quality and efficiency of the research.（1. 双方应充分发挥各自的资源优势（包括但不限于数据、技术及人力资源），共同推动项目顺利开展，确保研究的质量与效率。）​
2. The author cannot be included in the team of co-first authors or co-corresponding authors; in return, I myself am willing to provide unpaid support for other projects of the cooperating party as compensation. Relevant articles can be published jointly based on this database in the later stage, and the rights and interests of both parties in the articles shall be negotiated and determined in advance.（2. 部分作者无法加入共同第一作者或共同通讯作者团队；作为补偿，本人愿意为合作方的其他项目提供无偿支持。后期可基于本数据库联合发表相关文章，文章中双方的权益需提前协商确定。）​
3. For centers that provide a large sample size and high-quality data, one student from the center may be recommended to attend any one of my courses.（3. 对于提供样本量较大及数据质量较高的中心，可推荐1名学生任意参加本人的一门课程。）
